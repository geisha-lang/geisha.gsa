## 2. Key Machine-Checked Guarantees

1. **Sequential & Concurrent Type Safety (`¬ IsStuck`)**:
   Every active `Task` and `Actor` thread either terminates (as a value or defined `divByZero` trap), legitimately blocks on an unfulfilled `Task`/`MVar`/`Chan` synchronization primitive (`BlockedOnTask`), or takes a deterministic step.
2. **Elimination of `Trap.matchFailure` (`Exhaustive.lean`)**:
   Programs passing `checkProgramExhaustive` never evaluate to `Trap.matchFailure` (nor put any `Actor` into `.failed .matchFailure`) across arbitrary sequential or concurrent interleavings.
3. **Compile-Time Data-Race Freedom with `!Send`-State Actors (`Concurrency.lean`)**:
   `Actor s` is unconditionally `IsSend` even when `s` is `!Send` (`Cell τ` or `local` closures), because `actorSpawn` requires a `shared` `() => s` initializer (`exprLocs = []`), `actorCall`/`actorSend` require `shared` `s => r` handlers (`IsSend r`), and each actor processes its mailbox sequentially on a dedicated `ThreadId.actor aid`, preserving `¬ HasDataRace pool`.
4. **Stack-Frame Region Safety & Heap Refinement (`Escape.lean`)**:
   Stack-promoted `Cell` allocations freed eagerly upon function return (`popRegion`) never produce dangling stack dereferences (`¬ IsDanglingAccess`) and are simulated lockstep by heap semantics (`stack_alloc_simulates_heap`).
5. **Exact 40-Bit Pointer Masks & Sticky Generational Write Barrier (`GC.lean`)**:
   Immutable young allocations require zero write barriers; mutating heap containers with `gcWriteBarrierStore` preserves `GenHeapInv`; `minorGcCollect` preserves all reachable objects (`minorGc_sound`, whereas omitting the write barrier sweeps a live object in `missing_write_barrier_unsound`); `majorGcCollect` reclaims unreachable cyclic old-generation islands (`majorGc_complete`).
6. **Verification Condition Generator Soundness, Termination & Optimization Elision (`VCG.lean`)**:
   Programs passing `vcgen` / `verifyFn` with valid obligations never trap with `Trap.divByZero` and satisfy their `ensures` postconditions under `requires` and pairwise distinct mutable `Cell` arguments (`vcgen_sound`, `verifyFn_sound`), while `while_` loops with strictly decreasing non-negative ranking functions terminate in finitely many `Steps` (`while_decreases_terminates`). For verified functions under `ValidObs`, `CalleeSound`, satisfied `requires`, and non-aliasing initial heap (`StateMatches.loc_inj`), eliding runtime `assert` failure branches and `.divByZero` traps (`StepUnchecked` / `evalBinOpUnchecked` / `elideVerifiedAsserts`) is bidirectionally observationally equivalent to original `Steps` at the Core operational semantics layer (`verified_opt_sound`, while `unchecked_assert_false_diverges` and `unchecked_div_zero_diverges` witness unsoundness without verification); the compiler driver (`computeVerifiedSafeFns` in `src/verify.gsa`) enforces closed-world call-graph, non-aliasing parameter, and purity/no-`assume` preconditions before driving LLVM IR elision and `@llvm.assume` emission. Non-vacuity is witnessed by 4 concrete operational counterexamples (`Int32` wrapping overflow, truncating `%` & masked `<<`, missing loop havoc, and aliased mutable call arguments), and live root-sliced compiler `.gscore` dumps — including the compiler's own pure arithmetic/encoding/span/bitmap/HOF-pass kernels in-place inside `src/diag.gsa`, `src/codegen.gsa`, `src/codegen_wasmgc.gsa`, and `src/core.gsa` as well as mixed-module `verified def` units — are cross-verified end-to-end via `geisha verify --dump-core` + `geisha-check --verify-vcg`.
7. **Higher-Order Optimization, Positional Loop Specialization & Interface-Scoped Contextual Soundness (`OptSoundness.lean`)**:
   - **Local, Cloned-Body & Pipeline-Level Transformations (`ObsEquiv` / `ClosedObsEquiv` + `Φ ↦ Φ'` Extension)**: Selective left-to-right ANF lifting with skipped simple terms and preserved suffixes (`selectiveAnfDir_obsEquiv`, `selectiveAnfDir_open_obsEquiv`, `selectiveAnfApply_obsEquiv`), open pure and nested `TmMakeCls`-chain dead-`let` elimination (`coExprIsPure_closed_steps_to_val`, `coFinishLetWithDeadElim_coExprIsPure_obsEquiv`), `coCloneTerm` + `coOptTerm` binder α-renaming, `.block [e]` unwrapping, dead `let` dropping, and N-ary direct `TmVar` inlining (`AlphaOptRel`, `alphaOptRel_subst_closedObsEquiv`, plus sound rule-subset checker `checkAlphaOpt_sound` and partial executable cloner `coCloneOptExpr`), `CoEnv`-driven alias & known-value environment tracking (`CoEnvConsistent`, `coRecordLetBindingInEnvM_preserves_consistent`, `coResolveTermKnownValM_sound`) feeding 3-valued `coMatchPatKnownEnv` reduction (`coTryReduceMatchEnv_alphaClone_obsEquiv`), captures-first `$flat$` closure devirtualization with cloned/optimized bodies (`flat_closure_devirt_optBody_obsEquiv`), and positional multi-callback `$spec$` loop specialization across arbitrary callback indices, `.flatDir` / `.inline1` / N-ary inlined callbacks, `$co$`-renamed state & capture parameters, and in-body `TmMakeCls` closure reconstruction + call-site inlining + dead-wrapper elimination (`positional_hof_loop_spec_optBody_obsEquiv`, `inline1_reconstructed_cls_let_obsEquiv`, `specFn_body_makeCls_wrapper_obsEquiv` via `recFunBody_obsEquiv_congr`) preserve exact terminal values, runtime traps, and mutable `Cell` heaps.
   - **Verbatim Real-Artifact Verification & Interface-Scoped Contextual Equivalence (`ScopedCtxObsEquiv`)**: Alongside simplified positive/negative model witnesses, `OptSoundness.lean` proves operational equivalence directly on the verbatim `.gscore`-parsed `FunDef` ASTs emitted by the compiler on `adv.gsa` and `std.list.listFold`: `exact_artifact_bigCls_flat44_obsEquiv` (`makeLargeFlatRes.inner.closure.bigCls1$icls$29$flat$44` with `coCloneOptExpr` verified `rfl` on the 13-`let` body), `exact_artifact_specLoopColl_spec19_obsEquiv` (`specLoopColl$spec$19` with `.shared` source callback), `specFn_body_makeCls_wrapper_obsEquiv` (on the verbatim pre-`coOptTerm` `placeholderSpecLoopCollSpec19Fd` reconstructing `lam shared ($co$24$z)` and invoking `$co$21$f(2)` inside the recursive body), and `exact_artifact_listFold_spec18_obsEquiv` (2-argument `.shared` callback `std.list.listFold_Int_Int$spec$18` with no private `$flat$` helper left in `Φ2`).
